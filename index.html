The gap

A proof is a picture of the job, not the job.

It shows the art on a tilted product render, at the wrong scale, in the wrong colour space, with the imprint area drawn as a dashed box nobody can select. Turning that into a file your press accepts is manual work, on every single order.

  • Trace it, or eyeball dimensions from the callout
  • Isolate the art, strip the product mockup behind it

  • Convert colour, build a white underbase, choke it
  • A different result for every operator

How it works

It reads the proof the way your art lead does.

The hard part was never the export. It's finding the imprint area reliably across every layout a distributor invents, including the ones where the same box appears twice.

Try it on your proofs

  1. Find every candidate box

    Dashed borders, solid stroked rectangles, and separate 100% artwork pages are all recognised.

  2. Throw out the ones drawn in perspective

    Proofs repeat the imprint box on the tilted product render, skewed into a parallelogram. Picking that one is what makes art come out crooked, so only boxes square to the page survive.

  3. Lift the art, drop the mockup

    Background panels standing in for product colour, and fills close enough to white to be page rather than ink, are left behind.

  4. Write what the machine wants

    Spot channels, choked underbase, pure K vector, or an arc warp, depending on where the job is headed.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Which proofs does it read?

4imprint art proofs, solid-border proofs, and PO / distributor templates. If your distributor uses a layout we haven't seen, send it over and support for it gets added.

What files do I get back?

For screen print and heat transfer, a layered 700 DPI TIF with a named SPOT_1 white channel, plus a 300 DPI colour crop and underbase. For laser, a 1:1 vector .AI in 100% K. For cups, an arc-warped print PDF sized for 5, 12, 16 or 20 oz cups, or stadium cups.

Do my artists still need Illustrator?

Not for this step. The proof goes in and the production file comes out with no Illustrator session and no hand-traced imprint box. Your team can still open and tweak the output in their usual tools.

How does the demo work?

Fill in the form and we'll set up a short call. Bring a proof that gave your art department trouble and we'll run it live, so you see real output on real work before you commit to anything.

Is it per user or per file?

Neither. One flat price covers every tool, unlimited files and unlimited users.
